🔧 TROUBLESHOOTING

Q: Slow startup (> 30 seconds)
A: First run may pull images. Check:
   docker-compose logs

Q: Port already in use
A: Check what's on those ports:
   netstat -ano | findstr :8000
   netstat -ano | findstr :8001
   
Q: Container shows unhealthy
A: Check logs:
   docker logs th3-hexstrike

Q: Reports not generating
A: Verify in docker_config.json:
   "auto_reporting_enabled": true
   
Q: Vault sync not working
A: Check path exists:
   Test-Path D:\Vault\Vault\REPORT
