## 📝 How to Add Snippets in Pieces

### Step 1: Open Pieces App
- Click "+" button to create new snippet
- OR drag-drop code into Pieces

### Step 2: Fill Required Fields
```
Title: [name of snippet]
Description: [what it does, how to use it]
Code/Content: [paste the code/prompt]
Language: [select appropriate language]
Collection: [pick one of 4 collections above]
Tags: [add tags from list above - at least 2-3]
```

### Step 3: Save
- Click Save
- Snippet appears in collection
- Searchable by tag immediately
