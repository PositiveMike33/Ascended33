## 💎 3 Pricing Models

### MODEL 1: TIME-BASED (Hourly)

**Structure:** $150/hour × hours worked

**Calculation:**
```
Standard audit: 35 hours × $150/hour = $5,250
Quick audit: 20 hours × $150/hour = $3,000
Comprehensive: 50 hours × $150/hour = $7,500
```

**Pros:**
- ✅ Easy to explain
- ✅ Flexible for different scopes
- ✅ Fair to client if project grows
- ✅ Industry standard

**Cons:**
- ❌ Clients don't like uncertainty
- ❌ Incentivizes slow work (bad optics)
- ❌ Hard to estimate upfront
- ❌ Feels "transactional"

**When to use:**
- Very custom/unusual requirements
- Unclear scope
- Retainer relationships

**Conversion rate:** 20-30% (clients prefer fixed price)

### MODEL 3: VALUE-BASED (Outcome-focused)

**Structure:** Base fee + % of savings/risk reduction

```
Base fee: $3,500 (minimum)
PLUS: 15% of verified cost avoidance

Examples:
- If audit reveals $100K vulnerability
  (potential cost if exploited)
  → You earn: $3,500 + $15,000 = $18,500

- If audit reveals $50K at-risk expense
  (unnecessary cloud spend)
  → You earn: $3,500 + $7,500 = $11,000
```

**How to calculate "value":**
1. Current risk exposure (potential loss if breach)
2. Compliance fines avoided (PIPEDA = up to $10M possible)
3. Operational disruption prevention (ransomware downtime)
4. Cost efficiency gains (process improvements)
5. Risk reduction (insurance premium decrease)

**Pros:**
- ✅ Aligns your incentive with client value
- ✅ Premium pricing if you find big issues
- ✅ High-value outcome focus
- ✅ Can earn more than hourly/fixed

**Cons:**
- ❌ Hard to explain/justify
- ❌ Requires proof of value
- ❌ May scare off budget-conscious clients
- ❌ Negotiation-heavy

**When to use:**
- Enterprise clients with big budgets
- When you've identified major issues
- Retainer/ongoing relationships

**Conversion rate:** 30-40% (need trust first)

## 💡 Pricing Psychology

### Anchor effect
People compare prices to anchors. Your anchors:
```
"While major audit firms charge $25K-50K for similar services,
our SMB-focused offering is just $5,500 for equivalent quality."
```

This makes $5,500 feel like a bargain (even though it's still significant).

### Bundle effect
Include "extras" that look valuable but cost you $0:
```
Includes:
✅ 45-page professional report ($2K value)
✅ Executive summary ($500 value)
✅ Video walkthrough ($1K value)
✅ 90-day support ($1.5K value)
✅ Implementation worksheet ($500 value)
Total value: $5,500 (our price!)
```

Feels like they're getting everything included, not "just a report."

### Scarcity effect
Limit your capacity:
```
"I accept maximum 2 audits per month to ensure quality.
Next available slot: March 15th.
Would you like to schedule?"
```

Creates urgency without being pushy.
